Northwind Logistics: Employer Playbook

✓ VerifiedEmployer PlaybooksVerified by Care Ops Knowledge Team · Nov 2026

Use this card for any member whose employer is Northwind Logistics. Northwind's program differs from the standard Spring Health program in the ways below.

Sponsored care

  • Therapy: 12 sponsored sessions per member per plan year.
  • Medication management: 6 sponsored sessions per member per plan year.
  • Plan year: January 1 to December 31. Sessions reset on January 1.

After the sponsored sessions run out

Sessions are billed to the member's own insurance, and the member pays their plan's copay or coinsurance. This is expected behavior, not a billing error.

EAP session extension (Northwind only)

Northwind HR has pre-approved up to 6 additional sponsored therapy sessions per member per plan year through its EAP extension.

  1. Confirm the member has used all 12 sponsored therapy sessions this plan year.
  2. File an EAP Extension Request (billing correction ticket, type eap_extension). See EAP Session Extension Requests.
  3. Tell the caller the extension is requested, not approved. Northwind HR decides within 2 business days.
  4. Once approved, the extension covers sessions on or after the request date. Sessions already billed to insurance are not reversed.

Don't

  • Don't share Northwind's HR or benefits contacts with members or providers.
  • Don't promise the extension will be approved.
