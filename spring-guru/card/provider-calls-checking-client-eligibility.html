Provider Calls: Checking a Client's Eligibility

✓ VerifiedProvider SupportVerified by Provider Support Leads · Nov 2026

Providers call to confirm a client is eligible before booking, or to ask about what a client will owe.

Steps

  1. Verify the provider: full name and provider ID. The AI voice agent usually does this before the transfer; check the call context note.
  2. Identify the client: member ID and date of birth. Never search by name alone.
  3. Run the eligibility check from Decagon Assist (Actions → Eligibility check). Review the pre-filled fields, then Submit.
  4. Read back what the provider needs: eligible or not, sponsored sessions remaining, when they renew, whether the next session will be billed to insurance, and the cost estimate.
  5. If the employer has a playbook card (for example Northwind Logistics: Employer Playbook), follow it.
  6. Send the provider a written summary (the eligibility check can email it).

Never share with a provider

  • Diagnoses, clinical notes, or another provider's notes.
  • The client's employer contacts or plan documents.
  • Anything about other members.
